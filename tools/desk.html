How to proofread a page

Each page is shown whole: the scan, the page as a reader of the site will see it, and our transcription in wikitext. Compare the scan with the reader's view, correct the wikitext, and Save. When the whole page is right, press Save and mark page checked. Every save goes straight to your branch on GitHub: nothing is lost if you close the page.

Sending. When you want more pages, press Send checked pages and get more and confirm. Your saved pages go into the book on your branch, and the desk is rebuilt with your next pages. It takes a few minutes; the desk reloads itself when it is ready.

Flags. Words in yellow were caught by checks on the book's own order and notation, such as a headword out of alphabetical order or a mark not in the pronunciation key. Click a flag to find it in the wikitext. A flag is a pointer, not a verdict: the page may well print what was read.

Markup the edition reads otherwise (in red) is wikitext that will not come out on the site as it looks here, usually an apostrophe run left open or crossed (''italic'' and '''bold''' must close in the order they open) or a stray brace. Click it to find the line.

New lines. To start a new line within a paragraph, press Shift+Enter (or new line in the Markup palette); it types <br> for you. To begin a new paragraph, press Enter twice, leaving a blank line. A plain Enter alone, as on Wikisource, is read as a space: the printed lines join into one paragraph. For verse, or a list set line by line, put one printed line per line, select them, and press poem.

Quotation marks and apostrophes are curly, as the book prints them: use the Quotes keys (“ ” ‘ ’). A straight " pair typed by mistake still comes out curly, but a straight ' does not, and beside italic or bold ('' or ''') it is read as part of the markup.

Indents. Spaces typed at the start of a line do not show. To set a line in (verse), put indent from the Markup palette at its start.

Figures appear in the reader's view where they stand in the page.
