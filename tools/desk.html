How to proofread a page

Each page is shown whole: the scan, the page as a reader of the site will see it, and our transcription in wikitext. Compare the scan with the reader's view, correct the wikitext, and Save. When the whole page is right, press Save and mark page checked. Every save is kept, with the text before and after.

Flags. Words in yellow were caught by checks on the book's own order and notation, such as a headword out of alphabetical order or a mark not in the pronunciation key. Click a flag to find it in the wikitext. A flag is a pointer, not a verdict: the page may well print what was read.

Markup the edition reads otherwise (in red) is wikitext that will not come out on the site as it looks here, usually an apostrophe run left open or crossed (''italic'' and '''bold''' must close in the order they open) or a stray brace. Click it to find the line.

Lines. As on Wikisource, a single line break in the wikitext is read as a space: the printed lines join into one paragraph. Leave a blank line (press Enter twice) to begin a new paragraph. To keep lines as printed (verse, or a list set line by line), put one printed line per line, select them, and press poem in the Markup palette.

Your pages. The desk shows you the pages you fetched with your own key. To hand one to someone else, choose them under Give to and press Give: the page, with anything you typed on it, is theirs from then on. Show everyone's pages lets you read the others' pages; only a page's owner can save it.

Quotation marks and apostrophes are curly, as the book prints them: use the Quotes keys (“ ” ‘ ’). A straight " pair typed by mistake still comes out curly, but a straight ' does not, and beside italic or bold ('' or ''') it is read as part of the markup.

Indents. Spaces typed at the start of a line do not show. To set a line in (verse), put indent from the Markup palette at its start.

Figures appear in the reader's view where they stand in the page. If a crop misses part of the picture or takes in text, say so with Comment on this page.
